Plate 04 · ProcessTypical engagement: 3–6 weeks

From a rough idea to shipped screens.

Week 1

Audit

Map the users, the core job and every screen that exists today. Find where people stall.

Week 1–2

Direction

Show the core screen in two or three registers from the library. You pick one, or we blend.

Week 2–4

Prototype

Clickable flows with real copy, tested with real users before anything is final.

Week 4–6

Ship

Design tokens, components and production front-end code, handed over or built in.
